# B-AI Data Processing Addendum

**Last updated:** 13 November 2026

> Draft for review by the Owner. Not legal advice.

## 1. Who this addendum is between, and what it covers

This Data Processing Addendum ("**DPA**") is between:

- **D.S Spring Equity Ltd**, a company registered in Israel (company number 515616282), trading as B-AI, of 13 Zarchin Alexander, Raanana 4366241, Israel ("**B-AI**", "we", "us"); and
- the business that has accepted the B-AI Terms of Service (the "**Customer**", "you").

It forms part of the Terms of Service (https://b-ai-app.com/terms/) (the "**Terms**"). It applies whenever B-AI processes personal data in Customer Data on your behalf. You accept it when you accept the Terms. You do not need to sign it separately.

This DPA does **not** cover account data (for example Users' names, email addresses and sign-in data). For account data, B-AI is the controller, and our Privacy Policy (https://b-ai-app.com/privacy/) applies.

## 2. Definitions

Words that are not defined here have the meaning given in the Terms or in Data Protection Law.

- "**Data Protection Law**" means the laws that apply to the processing of Customer Personal Data under the Terms, which may include:
  - the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 ("**GDPR**");
  - the UK GDPR and the UK Data Protection Act 2018;
  - the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981, as amended (including Amendment 13), and the regulations under it, including the Privacy Protection (Data Security) Regulations, 5777-2017;
  - other data protection laws that apply to the processing.
- "**Customer Personal Data**" means personal data in Customer Data that B-AI processes on the Customer's behalf.
- "**Sub-processor**" means a third party that B-AI engages to process Customer Personal Data.
- "**Personal Data Breach**" means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data.
- "**SCCs**" means the standard contractual clauses approved by European Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914.
- "**UK Addendum**" means the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Commission Standard Contractual Clauses issued by the UK Information Commissioner under section 119A of the Data Protection Act 2018.
- "**controller**", "**processor**", "**data subject**", "**personal data**" and "**processing**" have the meanings given in the GDPR. Under Israeli law, "controller" includes the "database controller" (בעל שליטה במאגר) and "processor" includes the "holder" (מחזיק).

## 3. Roles

- The Customer is the **controller** of Customer Personal Data. Where the Customer acts for another controller, the Customer is a processor, and B-AI is its sub-processor.
- B-AI is the **processor** (under Israeli law, the holder) of Customer Personal Data.
- Each party will comply with the Data Protection Law that applies to it.

## 5. Customer instructions

- B-AI processes Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions. The Terms, this DPA, and the Customer's use and settings of the service are the Customer's complete instructions. Other instructions must be agreed in writing.
- B-AI may also process Customer Personal Data where the law requires it. In that case, B-AI will tell the Customer first, unless the law forbids it.
- B-AI will tell the Customer promptly if, in its opinion, an instruction breaks Data Protection Law. B-AI may then suspend carrying out that instruction.
- **Deletion requests through Meta.** The Customer instructs B-AI to act on deletion requests that people send to B-AI under Meta's data-deletion process, for data B-AI received through a Meta integration. B-AI will delete that data within 30 days and tell the Customer.

## 6. The Customer's obligations

The Customer:

- is responsible for the lawfulness of the processing, including having a lawful basis, giving notices to data subjects and getting any consent that is needed (for example for marketing messages);
- will make sure its instructions comply with Data Protection Law;
- is responsible for the accuracy and quality of Customer Personal Data, and for how it uses the service's settings and integrations;
- will review AI-generated configurations before relying on them, as the Terms require.

## 7. B-AI's obligations

B-AI will:

- process Customer Personal Data only as set out in section 5;
- make sure that everyone it authorises to process Customer Personal Data is bound by confidentiality;
- take the technical and organisational security measures in Annex 1, as Article 32 GDPR and the Israeli Data Security Regulations require;
- not sell Customer Personal Data, and not use it to train AI models or for targeted advertising;
- engage Sub-processors only under section 8;
- help the Customer with data subject requests under section 9;
- notify Personal Data Breaches under section 10;
- help the Customer with its obligations under Articles 32 to 36 GDPR (security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation), taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to B-AI;
- delete or return Customer Personal Data at the end of the service under section 12;
- make available the information needed to show compliance with this DPA, under section 13.

## 8. Sub-processors

- The Customer gives B-AI a **general authorisation** to engage Sub-processors.
- The current list is at https://b-ai-app.com/subprocessors/. The Customer approves the Sub-processors on that list.
- **Notice of changes.** B-AI will email the workspace owner **at least 30 days** before a new Sub-processor starts processing Customer Personal Data, and update the list.
- **Right to object.** The Customer may object in writing to a new Sub-processor within those 30 days, on reasonable data protection grounds. The parties will discuss the objection in good faith. If they cannot resolve it, the Customer may end the Terms by cancelling, and get a pro-rata refund of any fees it prepaid for the period after cancellation.
- **Emergency changes.** If B-AI must replace a Sub-processor urgently, for reasons outside its control (for example a security issue or the Sub-processor stopping its service), B-AI will give notice as soon as it can, and the Customer's right to object still applies.
- B-AI will put a written contract in place with each Sub-processor, with data protection obligations that protect Customer Personal Data at least as much as this DPA does, as far as they fit the service provided.
- B-AI stays responsible to the Customer for its Sub-processors' performance of these obligations.

## 9. Data subject requests

- The service gives workspace admins tools to **export** and to **erase** a person's data inside an application. The Customer will use these tools first.
- If B-AI receives a request from a data subject about Customer Personal Data, B-AI will pass it on to the Customer without undue delay, and will not answer it itself, except to tell the person that it has passed the request on, or as section 5 (Meta deletion requests) or the law allows.
- Where the Customer cannot answer a request with the tools, B-AI will give reasonable help, taking into account the nature of the processing.

## 10. Personal Data Breaches

- B-AI will notify the Customer **without undue delay, and in any case within 48 hours, after B-AI confirms** a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data.
- The notice will go to the workspace owner's email address. As far as the information is available, it will describe:
  - the nature of the breach, including the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned;
  - the likely consequences;
  - the measures taken or proposed to deal with the breach and to reduce its effects;
  - a contact point for more information.
- If not all information is available at once, B-AI will provide it in stages, without undue delay.
- B-AI will take reasonable steps to contain and investigate the breach and to reduce its effects.
- The Customer is responsible for notifying supervisory authorities and data subjects where Data Protection Law requires it. B-AI will give reasonable help.
- A notice under this section is not an admission of fault or liability.

## 11. Impact assessments and consultation

B-AI will give the Customer reasonable information and help for data protection impact assessments and prior consultations with supervisory authorities that relate to the service, as far as the Customer cannot get the information itself.

## 12. End of the service: return and deletion

- The Customer can export all Customer Data at any time.
- After cancellation or the end of the Terms, the workspace becomes **read-only**, and the Customer can export all Customer Data for free for **90 days**.
- B-AI sends a deletion notice **at least 30 days** before deletion.
- After the 90 days, B-AI **deletes all Customer Personal Data**, unless the law requires B-AI to keep it.
- Backups are kept for at most **30 days**, so deleted data leaves the backups within 30 days.
- B-AI keeps the ids of erased records (no content), so that an erasure is applied again if a backup is ever restored. The audit log keeps identifiers only (no content) for up to 7 years, as a legal record.
- On request, B-AI will confirm the deletion in writing.

## 13. Information and audits

- B-AI will make available, on request, the information reasonably needed to show compliance with Article 28 GDPR and this DPA. This includes written answers to reasonable security questionnaires, a description of its security measures, and its Sub-processor list. B-AI may share this information under confidentiality.
- The Customer may request this information **once in any 12 months**, or more often after a Personal Data Breach or where a supervisory authority requires it.
- If the documentation is not enough to show compliance, or a supervisory authority requires it, the Customer (or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality, who is not a competitor of B-AI) may carry out an audit. The Customer must give at least 30 days' notice, agree the scope with B-AI in advance, avoid disrupting the service or other Customers' data, and pay its own costs.
- Audits of Sub-processors take place through the reports and documents those Sub-processors provide.

## 14. International transfers

- Customer Data is hosted in the **EU** (Finland and Germany).
- B-AI is established in **Israel**. The European Commission and the UK recognise Israel as giving an adequate level of protection, so access from Israel needs no further safeguards under the GDPR or the UK GDPR while those decisions stay in force.
- Some Sub-processors process data in the **United States** (see the Sub-processor list). For those transfers, B-AI relies on:
  - the SCCs, Module 3 (processor to processor), in its contract with the Sub-processor, together with the UK Addendum for UK data; or
  - the Sub-processor's certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension, where it is certified.
- **Where SCCs are needed between the Customer and B-AI.** If a transfer of Customer Personal Data from the Customer to B-AI is a restricted transfer that no adequacy decision covers, the SCCs are incorporated into this DPA by reference, as follows:
  - **Module 2** (controller to processor) applies where the Customer is a controller, and **Module 3** (processor to processor) where the Customer is a processor;
  - the Customer is the data exporter and B-AI is the data importer;
  - Clause 7 (docking clause) applies;
  - Clause 9(a): Option 2 (general written authorisation), with the notice period in section 8 of this DPA;
  - Clause 11(a): the optional wording does not apply;
  - Clause 13: the supervisory authority is the one that is competent for the Customer under Clause 13(a);
  - Clauses 17 and 18: the law and courts of Ireland;
  - Annex I is completed with section 1 and section 4 of this DPA, Annex II with Annex 1 of this DPA, and Annex III with the Sub-processor list.
- **UK data.** For such transfers of personal data subject to the UK GDPR, the UK Addendum applies and is incorporated by reference. Its Tables 1 to 3 are completed with the information in this section and in the SCCs as set out above. For Table 4, either party may end the UK Addendum as allowed by its Section 19.
- **Israeli data.** Transfers of Customer Personal Data out of Israel are made under the Privacy Protection (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad) Regulations, 5761-2001.
- If there is a conflict, the SCCs and the UK Addendum take precedence over this DPA for the transfers they cover.

## 15. Liability

Each party's liability under or in connection with this DPA is subject to the exclusions and limits of liability in the Terms (section 14 of the Terms). This does not limit any liability to data subjects that cannot be limited under Data Protection Law, or under the SCCs.

## 16. Term, precedence and changes

- This DPA applies for as long as B-AI processes Customer Personal Data, including the periods in section 12.
- If this DPA conflicts with the Terms on the processing of Customer Personal Data, this DPA takes precedence.
- B-AI may update this DPA in the same way as the Terms. Changes will not reduce the overall protection of Customer Personal Data, unless the law requires it.
- This DPA is governed by the law and courts set out in the Terms, except where the SCCs or the UK Addendum require otherwise.
