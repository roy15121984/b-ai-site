## 2. What B-AI does

B-AI lets business owners describe their business in their own words. B-AI then builds a business system for them, such as a CRM, a recruitment system (ATS) or an operations and maintenance system, with records, automations and reports.

A business that signs up (a "**Customer**") gets a workspace. The Customer can invite team members ("**Users**"). The Customer stores data about its own customers, candidates and contacts in its applications.

## 3. Our two roles

We handle personal data in two different roles. It matters, because it decides who you should contact.

| Data | Examples | Our role | Who decides how it is used |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Account data** | Users' names, email addresses, sign-in data, usage data, and (later) billing data | **Controller** | B-AI |
| **Customer data** | Anything a Customer puts into its applications: records about its customers, candidates, contacts, employees, suppliers, files and notes | **Processor / service provider** | The Customer |

**If a business uses B-AI to hold your data** (for example, you applied for a job, or you are a client or lead of that business), that business is the controller. Please contact that business first. We help it answer your request. See section 10.

Most of this policy is about account data. Section 9 explains how we handle Customer data.

## 4. Account data we collect

## 7. The AI model provider

When a User describes a business or asks for a change, that text is sent to our AI model provider, Anthropic, so the product can generate or update the application.

- We use the provider under its commercial terms, under which the provider may not train models on the content we send it through its API.
- Under the provider's current data-retention terms, it deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, unless it must keep them longer, for example to enforce its usage policy or to comply with the law.
- Please do not put unnecessary personal data in these descriptions. Describe the structure of your business, not specific people.

## 8. International transfers

Our main hosting and backups are in the EU (Finland and Germany).

Some providers (for example the AI model provider, and possibly email providers) may process data outside the EU, UK or Israel, including in the United States. When personal data leaves the EU or UK for a country without an adequacy decision, we use safeguards such as:

- the European Commission's **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)**;
- the **UK International Data Transfer Addendum** to the SCCs, for UK data;
- where it applies, the provider's certification under the **EU-US Data Privacy Framework** (and its UK extension). At the time of writing, ActiveCampaign, LLC (Postmark) and Google LLC are certified. Anthropic is not, so for Anthropic we rely on SCCs and the UK Addendum.

The European Commission and the UK both recognise Israel as providing adequate protection. Transfers out of Israel follow Israel's transfer rules.

You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

## 11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the rights below. To use them, email **roy@b-ai-app.com**. We may need to confirm your identity first. We reply within the time the law sets (under the GDPR, usually one month).

### EU and UK (GDPR / UK GDPR)

- **Access** – get a copy of your data.
- **Rectification** – correct wrong or incomplete data.
- **Erasure** – ask us to delete your data.
- **Restriction** – ask us to limit how we use your data.
- **Portability** – get your data in a machine-readable format. Users can also export their data in the product.
- **Objection** – object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time.
- **Withdraw consent** – where we rely on consent, at any time. This does not affect earlier processing.
- **Complain** – to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular where you live or work or where you think the law was broken. In the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). We would be glad to hear from you first.

**EU and UK representative.** We are based in Israel and have no establishment in the EU or the UK. We are assessing whether we must appoint a representative in the EU (GDPR Art. 27) and in the UK (UK GDPR Art. 27). If we appoint one, we will add their details to this policy. Until then, please contact us directly at **roy@b-ai-app.com**.

### Israel (Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981, including Amendment 13)

- You may ask to **see** the information about you held in a database.
- You may ask to **correct or delete** information that is wrong, incomplete, unclear or out of date.
- You may ask to be **removed from direct-mail / marketing lists**.
- You may complain to the **Privacy Protection Authority** (הרשות להגנת הפרטיות).

**Database registration and Data Protection Officer.** Since Amendment 13, registering a database with the Privacy Protection Authority is required mainly for public bodies and for databases used to trade in personal data. Appointing a Data Protection Officer is required mainly for public bodies, data traders, and organisations whose main activity involves systematic monitoring of people or processing especially sensitive information on a large scale. On our current assessment, B-AI does not fall into these groups, so we have not registered a database or appointed a Data Protection Officer. We will review this as the service grows and update this policy if that changes. Privacy questions can always be sent to **roy@b-ai-app.com**.

### United States (state privacy laws, e.g. California)

Some US states, such as California, give residents privacy rights. Where these laws apply to us, you may have the right to:

- **know** what personal information we collect, use and disclose;
- **delete** personal information;
- **correct** inaccurate personal information;
- **opt out** of the sale or sharing of personal information, or its use for targeted advertising. We do not sell or share personal information for these purposes.

We will not discriminate against you for using these rights. You may use an authorised agent; we may ask for proof of the agent's authority. If we deny your request, you may be able to appeal by replying to our decision.

## 12. Security

We protect data with:

- encryption in transit (TLS);
- encryption at rest;
- tenant isolation enforced in the database, so one workspace cannot see another's data;
- multi-factor authentication (MFA) for privileged actions;
- an audit log of important actions;
- encrypted backups kept off the main servers.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 13. How long we keep data

**Customer data**

- A workspace owner can export all data and cancel at any time.
- After cancellation, the workspace becomes read-only. A free full export is available for **90 days**.
- After 90 days, **all Customer data is deleted**.
- We send a deletion notice **at least 30 days** before deletion.

**Backups.** Backups are kept for at most **30 days**. Deleted data leaves our backups within 30 days.

**Audit log.** The audit log keeps identifiers only (no content), as a legal record. We keep audit-log entries for up to **7 years**, the general limitation period for legal claims in Israel, and then delete them.

**Erasure log.** We keep the ids of erased records, so that if we ever restore a backup, the erasure is applied again.

**Account data.** We keep account data while the account is active, including the 90-day export period after cancellation, and delete it within 30 days after that. We keep some of it longer only where the law requires it or for legal claims. For example, billing and accounting records are kept for 7 years, as Israeli tax law requires.
