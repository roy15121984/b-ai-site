# B-AI Sub-processors

**Last updated:** 13 November 2026

> Draft for review by the Owner. Not legal advice.

B-AI is a trade name of D.S Spring Equity Ltd (Israel). This page lists the third parties that process personal data for B-AI. It forms part of our Data Processing Addendum (https://b-ai-app.com/dpa/).

We email workspace owners **at least 30 days** before a new sub-processor starts processing Customer data. Customers may object during that period, as the DPA explains.

## Sub-processors

| Sub-processor | Purpose | Data | Location | Transfer mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hetzner Online GmbH** (Gunzenhausen, Germany) | Hosting and databases; encrypted off-site backups (Hetzner Storage Box) | All Customer data and account data in the service; encrypted backups | EU (Finland and Germany) | None needed: data stays in the EU |
| **Anthropic PBC** (United States) | AI model that turns a User's description into an application configuration | The text a User types to build or change an application. It contains personal data only if a User types some in. | United States (data is stored in the US; requests may also be processed in other countries where Anthropic and its cloud providers operate) | EU Standard Contractual Clauses (2021/914) and the UK Addendum, in Anthropic's Data Processing Addendum. Anthropic is not certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. |
| **ActiveCampaign, LLC** (Postmark) (United States) | Transactional (service) email, such as invitations, security alerts and deletion notices | Recipient's email address and name, and the content of service emails | United States | EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension (ActiveCampaign, LLC is certified), and EU Standard Contractual Clauses in Postmark's Data Processing Addendum |
| **Google LLC** (Google Workspace) (United States) | Our own business email | Emails people send us and our replies. Not Customer data, unless a Customer sends it to us (for example in a support request). | United States and other countries where Google has data centres | EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension (Google LLC is certified), and EU Standard Contractual Clauses in Google's data processing terms |

**Payments.** We do not use a payment provider yet. We will add one to this list before paid plans start.

**Integrations the Customer turns on** (for example Meta lead ads, Google, WhatsApp or email) are not our sub-processors. Data flows to and from them on the Customer's instruction, under their own terms.

## Transfers from B-AI in Israel

B-AI itself operates from Israel. The European Commission and the UK recognise Israel as giving an adequate level of protection, so access from Israel needs no further transfer safeguards under the GDPR or the UK GDPR.
