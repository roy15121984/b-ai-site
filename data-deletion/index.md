# B-AI Data Deletion Instructions

**Last updated:** 13 November 2026

> Draft for review by the Owner. Not legal advice.

This page explains how to have your data deleted from B-AI. B-AI is a trade name of D.S Spring Equity Ltd (Israel).

Contact for all deletion requests: **roy@b-ai-app.com**

## 1. Data we received through Facebook or Instagram

Businesses that use B-AI can connect **Meta lead ads** (Facebook / Instagram). When you fill in a lead form on Facebook or Instagram for such a business, your answers (for example your name, email and phone number) are sent to that business's B-AI workspace.

That business decides how your data is used. B-AI stores it for the business.

**To delete this data, do either of the following:**

**Option A – Ask the business.** Contact the business whose form you filled in and ask it to delete your data. Its workspace admin can erase your data inside B-AI.

**Option B – Write to us.** Email **roy@b-ai-app.com** with the subject **"Data deletion request"** and include:

- your name;
- the email address and/or phone number you gave in the form;
- the business's name, or the page or ad, if you know it;
- approximate date you filled in the form, if you know it.

**What happens next**

1. We confirm we received your request.
2. We may ask for a little more information to find your data and confirm it is yours.
3. We tell the relevant business and **delete the data within 30 days**.
4. We **confirm by email** once the deletion is done.

Deleted data leaves our backups within 30 days, because backups are kept for at most 30 days. We keep a record of the deletion request and the ids of the erased records (no content), so the deletion is applied again if a backup is ever restored.



**Removing the app's access in Facebook.** You can also remove a connected app in your Facebook settings under *Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites*. This stops new data from being shared, but does not delete data already received. Use Option A or B for that. Meta changes these menus from time to time. If you connected B-AI to a Facebook Page or business account, it may appear under *Business integrations* instead.

## 2. Data a business stores about you in B-AI

If a business uses B-AI to hold your data (for example you are its client, lead or a job candidate), that business controls your data.

- Contact that business first and ask it to delete your data.
- If you cannot reach it, email **roy@b-ai-app.com**. We will pass your request on and help the business respond.

## 3. Your own B-AI account (Users)

If you are a User of B-AI:

- You can **export your data** in the product.
- To delete your user account, ask your workspace admin to remove you, or email **roy@b-ai-app.com**.

## 4. Deleting a whole workspace (workspace owners)

- A workspace owner can export all data and cancel in the product.
- After cancellation, the workspace is read-only, with a free full export for **90 days**.
- After 90 days, all workspace data is deleted. We send a notice at least **30 days** before.
- Backups are kept for at most 30 days.

The audit log keeps identifiers only (no content) as a legal record.
